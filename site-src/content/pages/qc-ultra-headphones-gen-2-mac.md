ANCBuddy 2.2.0 supports Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones 2nd Gen on macOS. The app covers frequent desktop controls while leaving Gen 2 firmware, Cinema Mode, deep customization, and unsupported settings in the Bose app.

## What works in ANCBuddy

With QC Ultra Headphones 2nd Gen, ANCBuddy provides:

- Quiet, Aware, and Immersion switching from the Mac menu bar
- Still and Motion when the connected headphones expose the setting
- battery and connection status
- active, connected, and remembered audio-source visibility
- switching to a remembered source
- starting and cancelling Bose pairing
- Mac-audio recovery when macOS exposes the Bose output
- optional track-aware AI Auto-EQ
- Launch at Login and built-in update checks

## What is specific to Gen 2

Bose documents Cinema Mode as a Gen 2 feature for clearer dialogue and spatialized video. The current ANCBuddy release does not advertise Cinema Mode control. Enable it in the Bose app instead.

Bose also documents additional Gen 2 hardware and app capabilities. ANCBuddy does not claim to reproduce every control merely because the headphones support it; the [Mac app decision page](/bose-qc-ultra-mac-app.html) lists the exact public scope.

## Set up the headphones on Mac

1. Pair QC Ultra Headphones 2nd Gen in **System Settings → Bluetooth**.
2. Start audio from the Mac and confirm the Bose output is selected.
3. Open ANCBuddy from the menu bar.
4. Switch a listening mode and confirm the headphones report the change.
5. Expand **Audio Sources** to see the active source and other connected or remembered devices.
6. Use the [troubleshooting guide](/troubleshooting.html) if audio and control states disagree.

## Gen 1 versus Gen 2 inside ANCBuddy

Both headphone generations receive ANCBuddy's supported core workflow for modes, source visibility and switching, pairing, battery, status, and optional AI Auto-EQ. Cinema Mode is available on Gen 2 hardware according to Bose, but it remains outside the current ANCBuddy control claim.

For source details and Multipoint terminology, use the [audio-source switching guide](/switch-bose-qc-ultra-audio-sources-mac.html). For Still, Motion, and Cinema, use the [Immersive Audio guide](/bose-qc-ultra-immersive-audio-mac.html).
