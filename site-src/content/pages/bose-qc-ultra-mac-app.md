ANCBuddy is a native Mac menu-bar app for Bose QC Ultra. Switch listening modes, see active and connected audio sources, switch to remembered devices, check battery status, and use optional AI Auto-EQ without opening the Bose phone app.

## Daily controls from the menu bar

Open ANCBuddy from the menu bar to reach the controls used most often at a Mac:

- switch Quiet, Aware, and Immersion
- switch Still and Motion inside Immersion on compatible devices
- check battery and connection status
- see active, connected, and remembered audio sources
- switch to a remembered source or start Bose pairing
- turn optional AI Auto-EQ on or off

## Audio sources without a phone detour

ANCBuddy 2.2.0 reads the source list remembered by the Bose QC Ultra. The panel highlights the active source, separates other connected sources, and lists remembered devices that are available for switching.

For the full workflow, including pairing and Mac-audio recovery, read [how to switch Bose QC Ultra audio sources from a Mac](/switch-bose-qc-ultra-audio-sources-mac.html).

## Active vs connected

**Active source** means the device currently streaming audio to the Bose QC Ultra. **Other connected sources** are still linked but are not the active stream. **Remembered devices** are saved in the Bose source list and may not currently be connected.

## Supported devices

ANCBuddy supports:

- Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones Gen 1
- Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones Gen 2
- Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds 2nd Gen

The app is focused on compatible Bose QC Ultra hardware; it is not a generic Bluetooth manager for every headphone brand.

## Limits

ANCBuddy does not advertise a Multipoint on/off toggle. It also does not replace the Bose app for firmware updates, initial product setup, hardware shortcuts, voice prompts, or settings not listed here.
